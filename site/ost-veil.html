How OST Veil Works

Offline Messages
These wait until the recipient comes online or gains access. They are delivered automatically when the user opens OST Veil — even if you are not connected to them right now.
Encrypted Messages
These are privacy-focused. They can be sent in both live and offline mode. Only the recipient can read them.
